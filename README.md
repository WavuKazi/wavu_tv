About WavuTV

WavuTV is the audience-facing application of the WavuKazi media ecosystem. It gives viewers access to programming provided by compatible WavuWebTv stations.

WavuTV is designed to connect to different independently operated WavuWebTv stations. The application is not permanently tied to a single WavuKazi station or deployment.

At this early release stage, tv.wavukazi.com is currently the available public WavuWebTv station for WavuTV. It serves as WavuKazi's demonstration and proof-of-concept station while the ecosystem is being introduced.

As additional compatible WavuWebTv stations become available, WavuTV can connect audiences to those stations as supported by the installed version.

WavuTV is more than a video player. It is the audience side of an ecosystem designed to connect viewers, broadcasters, creators, content distributors, and independently operated stations.

The goal is straightforward: make media a place where people can watch, participate, create, and build something of their own.

WavuKazi builds the tools people use to build with. WavuTV is one of those tools.

Features

- Schedule — View programming organized by a station's schedule.
- Featured — Explore programs highlighted by the station.
- Recorded — Access available recorded programming.
- Browse — Explore content offered through the connected station.
- Live viewing — Watch live broadcasts when supported and available.
- Content chat — Participate in conversations associated with supported content.
- Multiple station support — Connect to compatible WavuWebTv deployments rather than being permanently tied to one station.

Available features and content depend on the connected station, its configuration, your access permissions, and the capabilities of the installed version.

How WavuTV Works

WavuTV communicates with WavuWebTv. It does not connect directly to WavuDistro.

The application is designed so that a compatible WavuTV installation can connect to an available WavuWebTv station. Each station can operate independently and maintain its own programming, audience, content relationships, and business operations.

At the current proof-of-concept stage, WavuKazi operates the publicly available demonstration station at tv.wavukazi.com.

The ecosystem's media flow is:

Creators and Content Owners
           |
           v
       WavuDistro
           |
           v
       WavuWebTv
           |
           v
         WavuTV
           |
           v
         Audience

- WavuDistro provides content storage, organization, and distribution infrastructure.
- WavuWebTv operates the station, coordinates authorized content access, and delivers media to the audience application.
- WavuTV provides the viewer-facing experience.

This separation allows stations to present authorized programming without having to own or permanently store every piece of content they offer.

Current Demonstration Station

For this early release, viewers can connect to:

tv.wavukazi.com

This is WavuKazi's own demonstration WavuWebTv station and serves as the current proof of concept for the WavuTV → WavuWebTv architecture.

The existence of a single public station at launch does not mean WavuTV is designed for only one station. The application is designed around compatible WavuWebTv deployments, allowing the station ecosystem to expand independently of the audience application.

Designed for Participation

WavuKazi is built around the idea that participation can become opportunity.

A viewer may become a community participant, a live broadcaster, a recorded-content creator, or a content supplier. A creator may license work to a distributor. A distributor may supply programming to stations. A station may build an audience and develop its own media business.

A person may eventually encounter multiple stations through WavuTV, each independently operated and offering its own programming and audience experience.

WavuTV provides the audience-facing part of that larger system.

WavuKazi's purpose is to provide the tools and infrastructure that make these paths possible—not to claim ownership of the work people create.

Download and Install

1. Open the Releases section of this GitHub repository.
2. Open the release you want to install.
3. Under Assets, download the WavuTV Android APK.
4. Transfer the APK to your Android device if necessary.
5. Open the APK and follow Android's installation prompts.
6. If Android requests permission to install applications from your browser or file manager, review the request and allow it only if you trust the source.
7. Open WavuTV and connect to a compatible WavuWebTv station.
8. For the current release, the available public demonstration station is tv.wavukazi.com.

Security note: Install APKs only from WavuKazi's official distribution channels or another source you independently trust.

Requirements

- A compatible Android device.
- An internet connection.
- Access to a compatible WavuWebTv station.
- A station and account configuration that permits access to the requested content, where applicable.

Current availability: At this early release stage, WavuKazi's demonstration station at tv.wavukazi.com is the currently available public station.
